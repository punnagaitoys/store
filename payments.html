2. Transparent Pricing

All product prices at Punnagai Toy Store are displayed in Indian Rupees (₹). The price shown on our website catalogue and on our in-store price tags is the final retail price inclusive of all applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST). There are no additional gateway fees, service taxes, or processing charges added at checkout.

We strive to keep our prices accurate and up to date on our website; however, our in-store price is the definitive and binding price for any transaction. In the event of a discrepancy between the website price and the in-store price, the in-store price prevails. If you have questions about a specific product's price, please contact us via WhatsApp before visiting.

Prices are set to be fair and competitive for the quality of products and the personalised service we offer. We are a small, independent family store, and we appreciate your support in choosing us.

3. Pre-Booking & Counter Payment

Our WhatsApp Pre-Booking Service is completely free and requires no advance payment. When you pre-book a product via WhatsApp, you are reserving the item — not purchasing it. No deposit, booking fee, or advance payment of any kind is required or requested.

When you visit our Mylapore store to collect your pre-booked items, payment is completed by scanning our official shop counter UPI QR code.

Because no money changes hands at the time of pre-booking, there is no financial risk to you. If you decide not to collect or purchase the reserved item, simply let us know via WhatsApp — no cancellation charge will ever be applied.

We will never ask for personal bank transfers, gift cards, or payments to personal accounts. Official payments must always be made to our verified merchant UPI ID: MAB0451035A0089284@Yesbank.

5. GST (Goods & Services Tax)

All prices displayed on the Punnagai Toy Store website and in-store include applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST) as per current Indian taxation laws. GST on toys in India falls under standard government slabs, and our pricing is always fully GST-compliant.

The GST component of your purchase is included in the total price you pay — you will not be charged any additional tax at the time of purchase. The price displayed is the price you pay.

GST Invoice on Request

If you require a formal GST invoice for business purposes or accounting, please let our staff know when collecting your order at our counter. We will provide a GST-compliant tax invoice with our business details, itemised breakdown, and applicable tax percentages.

7. Payment Security & NPCI Protection

Your payment security is guaranteed through direct integration with the Unified Payments Interface (UPI):

  • NPCI Encryption: All UPI transactions take place directly on the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) encrypted network between your bank and Yes Bank.
  • No Third-Party Intermediaries: We do not route your funds through commercial payment gateways or card processors.
  • No Sensitive Data Collected: We never ask for, see, or store your UPI PIN, banking passwords, OTPs, or debit/credit card numbers. Your UPI PIN is entered solely within your own bank-authorized UPI app.
  • Fraud Protection: Verify that the payee name displayed in your UPI app says PUNNAGAI TOYS AND FANCY before entering your UPI PIN.
