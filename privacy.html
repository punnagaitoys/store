4. Cookies

Our website uses a small number of cookies to ensure the site functions correctly and to understand how it is used. A cookie is a small text file placed on your device when you visit a website. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

4.1 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: These are essential for the website to function. They include cookies that remember items in your wishlist or browsing session. Without these cookies, some features of the website may not work correctly. These cookies do not require your consent.
  • Functional Cookies: These remember your preferences (such as display settings) to improve your experience when you return to the site. They do not track your activity on other websites.
  • Analytics Cookies (if used): We may use privacy-friendly analytics tools to understand aggregate traffic patterns — for example, which pages are most visited. Any analytics we use are configured to anonymise your IP address and do not share your data with third-party advertising platforms.

4.2 What We Do NOT Do with Cookies

We do not use cookies for targeted advertising. We do not place cookies from third-party advertising networks (such as Google Ads, Facebook Pixel, or similar). We do not track your browsing activity across other websites.

4.3 How to Manage Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect the functionality of our website. For more information on managing cookies, visit www.allaboutcookies.org.

5. Data Sharing

Your privacy matters deeply to us. We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for their marketing or commercial purposes. We treat your data with the same care we give to selecting the toys on our shelves.

We may share your information only in the following very limited circumstances:

  • Payment Processors: If you use a card or digital payment method in-store, your payment is processed by our point-of-sale provider or UPI payment network. These processors are bound by their own data protection obligations and do not share your data with us beyond confirmation of the transaction.
  • Service Providers: We may use third-party service providers to help us operate our website (such as web hosting). These providers can only access your data to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by Indian law, court order, or governmental authority, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Punnagai Toy Store, our customers, or others.

In all cases, we limit the information shared to what is strictly necessary for the specific purpose.

6. Data Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously. We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, accidental loss, destruction, or disclosure.

Our website is served over HTTPS (secure, encrypted connection). WhatsApp communications are end-to-end encrypted by default. We limit access to personal information to only those staff members who need it to perform their job (such as processing your pre-booking request).

However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security. If you have reason to believe that your interaction with us is no longer secure, please contact us immediately using the details in Section 8.

We retain your personal data only for as long as it is necessary for the purposes it was collected — for example, order records may be kept for up to 3 years for accounting and legal purposes, after which they are securely deleted or anonymised.

7. Your Rights

You have certain rights regarding the personal information we hold about you. These include:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you. We will provide this within a reasonable timeframe.
  • Right to Correct: If any information we hold about you is inaccurate or incomplete, you have the right to ask us to correct it. Simply contact us via WhatsApp or email.
  • Right to Delete: You may request that we delete personal information we hold about you where it is no longer necessary for us to hold it, or where you have withdrawn your consent. Note that we may need to retain certain information for legal or accounting purposes even after your request.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where we process your data on the basis of your consent (such as marketing messages), you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal.
  • Right to Object: You have the right to object to processing of your personal data where we rely on legitimate interests as our legal basis for processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details provided in Section 8. We will respond to all requests within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.
