5. Pre-Booking via WhatsApp

Our WhatsApp Pre-Booking Service allows you to reserve a product at Punnagai Toy Store before visiting us in-store. This is a complimentary service designed to save you time and ensure the product you want is available when you arrive.

How It Works

  1. Browse our catalogue on the website and identify the product you wish to purchase.
  2. Send us a WhatsApp message at +91 75501 32101 / +91 72994 61657 with the product name, your name, and your intended collection date and time.
  3. Our team will confirm availability and reserve the item for you. You will receive a confirmation message via WhatsApp.
  4. Visit the store within the reservation window to purchase the item.

Reservation Hold Period

Pre-booked items are held for a maximum of 48 hours from the time of confirmation. If you are unable to collect within this period, please let us know in advance. Unreserved collections after 48 hours may result in the reservation being released.

No Advance Payment Required

No deposit or advance payment is required to make a pre-booking. Payment is made in full at the time of collection in-store. This means your reservation carries no financial commitment — if you decide not to purchase, simply let us know and there is no penalty.

Pre-bookings are subject to product availability at the time of your request. We cannot guarantee reservations during extremely high-demand periods (such as major festival seasons) unless confirmed by our team.
